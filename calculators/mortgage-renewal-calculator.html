Payments are calculated the Canadian way: the interest rate is compounded semi-annually and converted to an equivalent monthly rate. Amounts shown are principal and interest only and do not include property tax or insurance.

Your current payment is estimated from your balance, current rate and remaining amortization. Your actual payment may differ slightly depending on your lender's rounding and payment schedule.
