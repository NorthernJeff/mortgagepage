    Land transfer tax rebates and exemptions

    These provinces offer relief on land transfer tax. Each link opens the official government page with the full rules.

    • Ontario: Land transfer tax refunds for first-time homebuyers (up to $4,000)
    • City of Toronto: Municipal land transfer tax rebate for first-time buyers (up to $4,475)
    • British Columbia: First time home buyers' exemption
    • British Columbia: Newly built home exemption (any buyer, homes up to $1,150,000)
    • Quebec: Refundable tax credit for first-time buyers (up to $5,875, in French)
    • Prince Edward Island: Real property transfer tax first-time home buyers exemption

    Manitoba, New Brunswick and Nova Scotia don't offer a first-time buyer rebate. Alberta, Saskatchewan and Newfoundland and Labrador have no land transfer tax.

    This calculator gives estimates based on published 2026 rates for a home with one or two single-family residences. Some provinces base the tax on the greater of the price or the assessed value. Taxes for non-resident and foreign buyers, mortgage registration fees and legal fees are not included. Your lawyer or notary will confirm the exact amount.
