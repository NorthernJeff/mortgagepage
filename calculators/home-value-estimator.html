This is an estimate, not an appraisal

This estimate applies the change in Statistics Canada's New Housing Price Index for your area to your original purchase price. The index tracks builders' prices for new houses, not resale homes or condominiums, and it does not reflect your home's condition, age, renovations, location or recent local sales.

Use it only as a ballpark figure. Only a professional appraisal can determine your home's market value. If you sell or refinance, your real estate agent or the lender's appraiser will determine the actual value.
