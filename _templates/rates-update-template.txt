MORTGAGEPAGE.CA - RATES UPDATE
==============================

How to use:
1. Fill in the rate for each term (for example 4.44). Leave a rate blank to show a dash.
2. Enter the lender, broker or brokerage shown beside each rate.
3. If a lender is new, attach its logo image (PNG or JPG) to your message with this file.
4. The Inquire link is optional. Leave it blank until you have one.
5. Send this file (or paste it) to Claude and ask to update the rates on MortgagePage.ca.
   The "Rates last updated at" time is set automatically when Claude makes the update.
